To contact iStudix support, email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai). Our team reads every message and will get back to you as soon as possible.

## What to include

To help us solve your problem faster, include:

- The email address linked to your iStudix account
- What you were trying to do and what happened instead
- Any error message you saw
- A screenshot, if possible
- Your browser and device

<Tip>
  Before you write, check [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/support/faq). Your answer may already be there.
</Tip>
