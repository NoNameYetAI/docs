Use this documentation site for product guides. For help with your account or a specific problem, open **Help & Support** in iStudix to contact the team and follow your request.

## Open help & support

1. Sign in to [iStudix](https://www.istudix.app/).
2. Open the account menu by selecting your name or avatar in the sidebar.
3. Select **Help & Support**.

The app opens the support page without leaving iStudix. The **How can we help you?** panel includes a **Visit our support page** link that opens this documentation site in a new tab.

## Create a support ticket

When tickets are available, use **Create a Support Ticket** on the support page:

1. Select a category: billing, account, technical issue, feature request, general question, or other.
2. Enter a short subject that describes your request.
3. Describe the problem in the message field. The initial message allows up to 1,000 characters.
4. Optionally attach a file. The attachment limit shown in the app is 5 MB.
5. Submit the ticket.

<Note>
  If the ticket form is not available, you can still consult the documentation or email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai).
</Note>

## Follow your request

Your requests appear under **My Tickets**. Filter by status and use the page controls to browse the list. Select a ticket to read its details and conversation with the support team.

Tickets can be **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved**, or **Closed**. For open or in-progress tickets, add a reply to provide more information or answer the team's questions. Replies are disabled for resolved or closed tickets; contact support again if you need further help.

## Contact by email

You can also email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai), including when you cannot sign in to the app.

## What to include

To help us solve your problem faster, include:

- The email address linked to your iStudix account
- What you were trying to do and what happened instead
- Any error message you saw
- A screenshot, if possible
- Your browser and device

Do not include passwords, login codes, payment card details, or other secrets. Check screenshots and attachments for sensitive information before sending them.

<Tip>
  Before you write, check [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) and the [FAQ](/support/faq). Your answer may already be there.
</Tip>
