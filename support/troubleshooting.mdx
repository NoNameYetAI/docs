---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Fix common iStudix problems: can't sign in, the site won't load, uploads fail or get stuck, or the AI assistant is slow or gives poor answers."
---

Most iStudix problems are solved by refreshing the page, checking your internet connection, and making sure you're using [**www.istudix.app**](http://www.istudix.app). Find your symptom below for specific fixes.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The site won't load">
    Make sure you typed the full address with `www`: [https://www.istudix.app/](https://www.istudix.app/). Then refresh the page, or try a different browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't sign in">
    Check that you're using the same sign-in method and email you used to sign up. Clear your browser cache or try a private window. If you still can't sign in, contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My upload failed or is stuck">
    Check your connection and confirm the file opens on your device. Try uploading again, and for large files allow extra time to process. See [Upload your study materials](/using-istudix/upload-materials).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI assistant is slow or gives poor answers">
    Make sure your material finished processing. Ask specific questions, and keep related materials in the same [notebook](/using-istudix/notebooks).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If that didn't work

Email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai) and include your account email, what you were trying to do, any error message, and a screenshot if possible.
