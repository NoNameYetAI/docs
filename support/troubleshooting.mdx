---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Find the next step for sign-in problems, queued or failed processing, insufficient credits, and unexpected AI answers."
---

Find the symptom below and check the status shown in [the iStudix app](https://www.istudix.app/). This documentation explains recovery steps; it cannot resume jobs or change your balance.

## Before you start

Note the material or study-tool name, its status, and the exact error. Check your connection and that you are in the correct account and notebook. Before refreshing during a study attempt, check its save status. Do not repeatedly upload a file or submit generation to make a pending job finish.

## Steps

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The site won't load">
    Make sure you typed the full address with `www`: [https://www.istudix.app/](https://www.istudix.app/). Then refresh the page, or try a different browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't sign in">
    Use the same sign-in method and email as when you registered. If the app opens email verification, follow the link in your inbox and sign in again. Check spam and use the resend action when available. For a forgotten password, follow [password recovery](/getting-started/sign-in). If needed, try a private window before clearing browser data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My material or study tool is queued or processing">
    Queued work is waiting to start; processing work has not finished yet. Wait and check **Materials**, the study-tool list, or background activity in the app. Do not create duplicates. Processing time varies, and saving a file or submitting generation is not confirmation that it is ready. If the status does not progress and you need help, contact support with the name and approximate submission time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Processing is paused for insufficient credits">
    Open **Credits** in the app header and check **Available** and **Reserved**, then review the action's estimate if available. Active AI requests can reserve credits, reducing the balance available for other work. An estimate is not a fixed charge; the reservation can require more available credits than the estimated consumption. Completed work is charged even if later processing pauses.

    Check your [current plan and allowance](/account/plans-and-billing). When enough credits are available, use **Resume processing** only if the paused job offers it in background activity. Check the job's status afterward. More credits do not guarantee automatic resumption or instant completion. If no resume action is available, or resuming fails, contact support rather than submitting duplicates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An upload or generation job failed">
    Read the error before taking another action. For a material, confirm the original file opens and meets the formats shown in the upload form. For a study tool, confirm its selected materials are ready. Resolve the reported cause, then use a retry action only if the app provides one. Retrying does not guarantee that earlier usage is refunded. See [upload guidance](/using-istudix/upload-materials) or contact support if the failure continues.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI assistant is slow or gives poor answers">
    Make sure your material finished processing and the Tutor has the correct [notebook](/using-istudix/notebooks) selected. Ask a specific question and verify the answer against your original material. AI answers can be incomplete or wrong. Read any error before sending another request; new AI requests may use more credits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What happens next

Check the status after the recovery action. Wait for the material or tool to be ready before studying with it. If the same error returns, stop repeating the action and ask for help.

## Need help?

Open **Help & Support** in the app, [contact support](/support/contact), or email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai). Include your account email, notebook and item names, approximate time, what you tried, and the exact error. Add a screenshot if useful, hiding private content, payment details, passwords, and login codes.
