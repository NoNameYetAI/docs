Quick answers to the questions we hear most about iStudix. Each answer links to a full guide when there's more to know.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is iStudix?">
    iStudix is an AI learning assistant. You upload your study materials, work with them in notebooks, practice with flashcards, quizzes, and exams, plan study sessions, and track your progress, all in one app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get started?">
    Sign up at [www.istudix.app](https://www.istudix.app/), upload a study material, and open it in a notebook. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does iStudix cost?">
    See the [pricing page](https://www.istudix.app/#precos) for current plans and prices. Learn how to change plans in [Plans and billing](/account/plans-and-billing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can I upload?">
    Upload documents and study materials such as notes or textbook chapters. Clear, text-based files work best. See [Upload your study materials](/using-istudix/upload-materials).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is my data handled?">
    Read our [Privacy Policy](https://www.istudix.app/privacy) for details on how iStudix collects and protects your data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel or delete my account?">
    Cancel your plan from **Settings**. To delete your account, email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai). See [Account settings](/account/settings).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai). See [Contact support](/support/contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
