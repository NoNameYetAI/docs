---
title: "What is iStudix"
description: "iStudix is an AI learning assistant that turns your study materials into notebooks, flashcards, quizzes, and exams, with a calendar to track exam dates."
---

iStudix is an AI learning assistant that helps you study smarter. You upload your own study materials, and iStudix organizes them into notebooks where an AI Tutor answers your questions, creates flashcards, and builds quizzes and practice exams from your content.

## How iStudix works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a notebook">
    Create a notebook for a subject and set your exam date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your materials">
    Upload the documents you want to study from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Study with AI">
    Ask the AI Tutor questions, review flashcards, and take quizzes and practice exams based on your own materials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who iStudix is for

iStudix is for students preparing for exams and certifications.
