The iStudix dashboard is your home screen after you sign in. From here you can reach every area of the app: upload materials, open notebooks, plan sessions, and track your progress.

## Main areas

| Area | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | See an overview of your recent activity and jump back into your work. |
| **Upload** | Add documents and study materials for the AI assistant to work with. |
| **Notebooks** | Study your materials, take notes, and keep topics organized. |
| **Calendar** | Plan and schedule study sessions. |
| **Progress** | See how your learning is going over time. |
| **Settings** | Update your profile, preferences, and account. |

<Tip>
  Lost? You can always return to the dashboard by clicking the iStudix logo.
</Tip>
