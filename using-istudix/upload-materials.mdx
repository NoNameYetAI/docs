---
title: "Upload materials"
description: "How to upload documents, notes, and study materials to iStudix so the AI learning assistant can help you study, summarize, and review them."
---

To upload a study material, open a notebook and its **Materials** tab, then choose the action to add material. Select a file, save it to the notebook, and wait for processing. Once it is ready, you can use it with the AI Tutor and study tools.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your notebook">
    Open **Notebooks**, select or create a [notebook](/using-istudix/notebooks), and open **Materials**. Choose the action to add material.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your file">
    Select a file from your device, or drag and drop it into the upload area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and wait for processing">
    Review the material options and save. Check its processing status in **Materials** before generating study tools. Larger files can take a little longer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start studying">
    Open the material in a [notebook](/using-istudix/notebooks) to start working with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Clear, text-based documents give the AI assistant the best results. Scanned or blurry pages may be harder to read.
</Tip>

## Upload not working?

If an upload fails or seems stuck, check your internet connection, confirm the file opens on your device, and try again. See [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) for more fixes.
