title: "Your first study session"
description: "Learn how to sign up for iStudix, upload your first study material, and start learning with the AI assistant in four quick steps."
---

You can go from sign-up to studying with iStudix in about five minutes: create an account, upload a study material, open it in a notebook, and start asking the AI assistant questions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [www.istudix.app](https://www.istudix.app/) and sign up. See [Create your account](/getting-started/create-account) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a study material">
    From your dashboard, open **Upload** and add a document you want to study, such as lecture notes or a textbook chapter. See [Upload your study materials](/using-istudix/upload-materials).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a notebook">
    Go to **Notebooks** to work with your material and keep your notes in one place. See [Create and organize notebooks](/using-istudix/notebooks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan and track your study">
    Schedule sessions in the **Calendar** and check **Progress** to see how you're doing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
