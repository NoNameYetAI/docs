Start studying with iStudix by creating an account and a notebook, adding a study material, and asking the AI Tutor questions once processing finishes.

## Before you start

Have your account ready and choose one short material you have permission to use. This guide explains the workflow; complete the actions in [the iStudix app](https://www.istudix.app/), not on this documentation site. Processing and AI generation may require credits. Review any estimate shown before submitting an action.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [www.istudix.app](https://www.istudix.app/) and sign up. See [Create your account](/getting-started/create-account) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a notebook">
    Open **Notebooks** and create a notebook for your subject. See [Create and organize notebooks](/using-istudix/notebooks).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a study material">
    Open the notebook's **Materials** tab and add a document, such as lecture notes or a textbook chapter. Save it and wait for processing before asking the Tutor about it. See [Upload your study materials](/using-istudix/upload-materials).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that your material is ready">
    Check its status in **Materials**. A queued or processing material is not ready yet. If it pauses for insufficient credits, follow [troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) rather than uploading the same material repeatedly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a study tool">
    Open **Flashcards** or **Quizzes** inside the notebook. Choose the available material, review the generation options and any credit estimate, then submit once. Wait for the new deck or quiz to finish processing. See [Flashcards](/using-istudix/flashcards) or [Quizzes and exams](/using-istudix/quizzes-and-exams).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practise and finish your session">
    Open the ready deck or quiz. For flashcards, try recalling the answer, flip the card, and rate your confidence. For a quiz, choose your answers. Finish the session using its available completion action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and plan your next session">
    Review your results in the notebook's **Dashboard** tab and return to materials you found difficult. Use **Calendar** to keep track of study and exam dates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Your notebook keeps your materials and generated study tools together. You can reopen them for another study session; creating a new tool is a separate action and may require more credits. See [Progress](/using-istudix/progress) for how to interpret your results.

## Need help?

Read [troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) for processing or credit errors. If you still need help, open **Help & Support** in the app or [contact the team](/support/contact). Never send passwords or login codes.
