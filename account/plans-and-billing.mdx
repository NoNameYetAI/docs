You can see all iStudix plans and current prices on the [pricing page](https://www.istudix.app/#precos). To upgrade, change, or cancel your plan, go to **Settings** in the app.

## Upgrade or change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Compare plans">
    Review the options on the [pricing page](https://www.istudix.app/#precos).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the app, open **Settings** and find your plan and billing details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select the plan you want and complete payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel your subscription

Open **Settings**, go to your plan details, and cancel your subscription. If you can't find the option, email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai) and we'll help.

## Billing questions

For invoices, refunds, or payment problems, contact [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai) with your account email. See our [Terms of Service](https://www.istudix.app/terms) for billing terms.
