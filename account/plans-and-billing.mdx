To review or manage your plan, open your name or avatar menu in the app, choose **Account Settings**, then **Subscription**. You can also compare options on the [pricing page](https://www.istudix.app/#precos). Use the current plan details and checkout shown in the app as the source of truth for available options, prices, and billing terms.

## Usage, credits, and limits

AI usage, available credits, storage, and limits for notebooks and study tools depend on your selected plan. Check the current plan details in **Subscription**, your usage in **Account Settings**, and the credit estimate shown before an AI action. No plan should be assumed to include unlimited usage.

If you cannot upload materials or generate a study tool, read the message shown in the app and check your usage and plan allowances. Contact support if the displayed plan or allowance does not match what you expect.

## Upgrade or change your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Compare plans">
    Review the available plans and billing cycles in **Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open subscription settings">
    Open your name or avatar menu, choose **Account Settings**, then **Subscription** to see your current plan and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Choose an available upgrade and review the checkout details before completing payment. Return to **Subscription** to check the updated status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancel your subscription

Open **Account Settings** → **Subscription**. If a billing-management action is available for your subscription, open it and follow the cancellation options in the billing portal. Review any effective date and terms shown there. If you cannot find the option, [contact support](/support/contact) or email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai).

## Find invoices

Check the payment history in **Account Settings** → **Subscription**. When an invoice PDF is available, use its download link. Contact support if you need an invoice that is not shown.

## Billing questions

For invoices, refund questions, or payment problems, [contact support](/support/contact) or email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai) with your account email. Do not send payment card details. See our [Terms of Service](https://www.istudix.app/terms) for billing terms.
