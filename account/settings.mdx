To change your profile, preferences, or sign-in details, open your name or avatar menu and choose **Account Settings**. Use the relevant section for profile, notifications, appearance, privacy, or subscription settings.

## Update your settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    Open your name or avatar menu and choose **Account Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update your profile details and preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete your account

1. Open **Account Settings** and select **Data & Privacy**.
2. Choose the account-deletion action in the danger section.
3. Read the warning, type `DELETE`, and confirm only when you are ready to permanently delete your account.

If you cannot sign in or complete deletion, email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai) from the address linked to your account. See our [Privacy Policy](https://www.istudix.app/privacy) for how we handle your data.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account permanently removes your uploads, notebooks, and progress.
</Warning>
