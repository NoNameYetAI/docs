---

To change your profile, preferences, or sign-in details, open your name or avatar menu and choose **Account Settings**. Use the relevant section for profile, notifications, appearance, privacy, or subscription settings.

## Before you start

Sign in to the account you want to change. Settings are changed in the app, not on this documentation site. Keep access to your account email if you need to recover your password.

## Steps

### Update your settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    Open your name or avatar menu and choose **Account Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Choose **Profile** for personal details, **Notifications** for reminders, or **Appearance** for display preferences. Use **Subscription** for [plan and billing information](/account/plans-and-billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Use the section's save action when shown and check for confirmation or an error before leaving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delete your account

1. Open **Account Settings** and select **Data & Privacy**.
2. Choose the account-deletion action in the danger section.
3. Read the warning, type `DELETE`, and confirm only when you are ready to permanently delete your account.

If you cannot sign in or complete deletion, email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai) from the address linked to your account. See our [Privacy Policy](https://www.istudix.app/privacy) for how we handle your data.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account permanently removes your uploads, notebooks, and progress.
</Warning>

## What happens next

Return to the relevant section to check your saved preferences. Notification delivery also depends on device permissions; enabling a preference does not send an immediate reminder. Confirm account deletion only when you no longer need access to the account's study content.

## Need help?

If a change fails, read the error and check the field requirements before saving again. For password recovery, follow [sign-in help](/getting-started/sign-in). If you still cannot save a change, [contact support](/support/contact) with the section name and error, not your password.
