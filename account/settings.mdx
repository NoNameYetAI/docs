title: "Account settings"
description: "How to update your iStudix profile, change your preferences, manage your password and sign-in, and request deletion of your account."
---

To change your profile, preferences, or sign-in details, open **Settings** from your dashboard. Changes are saved to your account and apply everywhere you use iStudix.

## Update your settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    From your dashboard, click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update your profile details and preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete your account

To delete your account and your data, email [mail@justgenius.ai](mailto:mail@justgenius.ai) from the address linked to your account. See our [Privacy Policy](https://www.istudix.app/privacy) for how we handle your data.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account permanently removes your uploads, notebooks, and progress.
</Warning>
